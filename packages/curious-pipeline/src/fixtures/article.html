How Mithun’s Jimmy Jimmy became a Soviet spy test

Disco Dancer, released in 1982, drew about 60.9 million admissions in the Soviet Union, according to Soviet distribution records.

It is believed that the former Soviet government could identify spies with the song: say "Jimmy Jimmy" and anyone raised in the USSR would reportedly answer "Aaja Aaja" without thinking.

Decades later, the song is still played at weddings.
Mithun Chakraborty, now 76, says he never expected it.
