Ten laps in DRS range, and then Russell

Max Verstappen led 62 of the first 63 laps. Lando Norris spent ten of them less than a second behind him. Neither won.

Lap 1

The front row holds. Leclerc falls to 18th

Verstappen led away from pole and Norris kept second, so the first lap changed nothing at the front. Behind them Hamilton took fourth from Sainz, and Perez gained two places to sixth.

Leclerc started sixth. He pitted at the end of the opening lap and rejoined 18th, the only driver to lose more than one place.

Laps 1–50

Verstappen's lead grows to eight seconds

Norris was 1.3 seconds behind after the first lap and 4.4 seconds behind after ten. By lap 20 it was 5.8.

Both stopped on lap 23 and spent almost the same time in the pit lane: 21.8 seconds for Verstappen, 21.6 for Norris. The stops changed nothing between them.

For one lap Piastri, who stayed out two laps longer, led the race. He stopped on lap 25 and Verstappen was back in front.

On hard tyres the gap kept growing, to 8.1 seconds on lap 40.

Lap 51

A slow stop hands Norris almost six seconds

Both leaders stopped again on lap 51. Norris was through the pit lane in 21.4 seconds. Verstappen took 25.8.

Time in the pit lane, seconds

Pit lane times, OpenF1.

A lap later the gap was 1.8 seconds, down from 7.6. For the first time since the start, Norris could see the Red Bull.

Laps 53–63

Ten laps inside a second

From lap 54 Norris crossed the line less than a second behind Verstappen every lap: close enough for DRS, not close enough to stay past.

Norris's gap to Verstappen, laps 52–63

Gap at the line from cumulative lap times, FastF1. Dashed: one second.

Their lap times were all but the same. On lap 58 Verstappen did a 1:09.183 and Norris a 1:09.161; on lap 62, 1:09.528 and 1:09.509.

By the end of lap 63 the gap was 0.49 seconds, the closest it had been.

Two cars, ten laps, never more than a second apart.

Lap 64

The lap it came apart

Lap 64 took Verstappen 1:38.0, 28 seconds slower than the lap before, and Norris 2:00.3. Both came into the pits at the end of it. Norris never came back out.

Russell had been third and 15.8 seconds behind after lap 63. One lap later he was leading the race.

Verstappen rejoined fourth, on soft tyres, and finished fifth.

Lap 65

Piastri takes second into Turn 6

The fight for second was the race's last pass at the front. Piastri was 0.7 seconds behind Sainz at the end of lap 64, and ahead of him before the end of lap 65.

A virtual safety car on lap 66 slowed the field for a lap, then three cars were left to settle the podium.

Laps 64–71

Piastri gets within two seconds, and no closer

Piastri was 2.9 seconds behind Russell when Russell took the lead. He took a second out of that in the last seven laps.

The fastest lap of the race came from further back: Alonso, 1:07.694 on lap 70.

Lap 71 · Result

Russell by 1.906 seconds

Russell won from third on the grid. Piastri finished second from seventh and Sainz third. Verstappen, on pole, was fifth, 37 seconds behind.
