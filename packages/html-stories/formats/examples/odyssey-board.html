Told out of order

The poem opens in year ten, on Calypso’s island. The monster tales are a flashback, told by Odysseus at a banquet. The map every reader builds was drawn from memory, by the man who got lost.

how long should it take? before the wind: places we can find the cost

  1. Ten years, pinned to one board

    Odysseus’s voyage home from Troy, laid out like a case file. Follow the tour, or drag the board and zoom in on anything.

  2. A two-week trip

    Troy to Ithaca is 565 nautical miles. A Bronze Age galley could do it in a week or two. Homer takes ten years.

  3. Where the years went

    Seven years with the nymph Calypso and one with the sorceress Circe. The adventures everyone remembers fit in a single sailing season.

  4. Told out of order

    Homer hides the arithmetic by starting in year ten and letting Odysseus tell the middle himself.

  5. The route, as tradition draws it

    Each string runs from a landfall to the place tradition puts it. Only the solid gold stretch is geography we can stand on.

  6. The hinge

    Troy and Ismaros are excavated. Then a north wind catches him off Cape Malea, and from there every landfall has rivals.

  7. Five rival maps

    Severin keeps him near Greece. Bérard sends him to Gibraltar. Eratosthenes thought the search was hopeless two thousand years ago.

  8. The cost

    Twelve ships and about 720 men leave Troy. One man comes home. A single harbour, the Laestrygonians’, takes eleven ships.

  9. Was it ever real?

    Even home is contested. Bittlestone put Homer’s Ithaca on Paliki; in 2026 Diggle and Underhill answered that it was no island at all.

  10. The wine-dark sea

    A colour we can all picture and none of us can quite name.
