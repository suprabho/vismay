## Long version (blog / LinkedIn / newsletter)

**Is AI booming or dooming today? We built a scale that answers that every morning.**

Every day brings another headline that says the AI build-out is unstoppable, and another that says it's about to hit a wall. A gigawatt campus gets announced. A county freezes permits. HBM is sold out through 2027. A grid operator warns it can't connect new load. If you follow this space, you've probably felt the whiplash.

So we built **AI Daily**, a single frozen edition every morning with one question at the top: **Doom or Boom?**

**How the scale works**

We read the previous 24 hours of news on AI data centers, hyperscalers, chips and chip equipment. Each story gets tagged boom (it pushes the build-out forward), doom (it holds it back) or neutral.

Counting headlines would be too easy to game, so we don't do that:

- **One event counts once.** If twelve outlets cover the same power deal, that's one event, not twelve votes.
- **Events carry different weights.** Each one is weighted by how central it is to the AI build-out, how much it actually moves things, and how widely it was covered (with a cap, so the loudest story can't drown out the rest).
- **The reading is the balance.** Boom weight minus doom weight, divided by the total, gives a needle between −1 (decisively doom) and +1 (decisively boom). Ghost ticks show the 7- and 30-day averages, so you can see whether today is unusual or part of a trend.

And you can trace every number. Tap either side of the meter to see the exact events behind it, with links to every source.

**What else is in an edition**

After the mood reading, the edition looks at the day by geography, by AI layer (data centers, hyperscalers, semiconductors, equipment), in new research from arXiv, and through AI + energy. It's composed at 06:15 UTC, published at 09:00 UTC, and never edited after that. Corrections run in the next edition, and every past edition stays online.

**Help us choose the sources**

Right now the feed is built from broad Google News searches on AI data centers, chips and hyperscalers, arXiv papers (cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.LG, cs.CV, cs.AR, cs.DC) and IEA energy news.

We want to widen it, and we'd like your input on where to look. Some examples:

- Trade press you trust on power, grid or data-center construction
- Utility filings, grid-operator queues, or local planning and permitting notices
- Company disclosures: earnings calls, 10-Ks, sustainability reports
- Regional outlets outside the US that cover builds we're missing
- Research labs, newsletters or analysts you read every week

**Which sources would you add? Reply or comment with your picks.** The most-requested ones will go into the feed first.
