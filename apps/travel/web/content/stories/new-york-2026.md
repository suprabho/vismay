# New York

*NoHo · Greenwich Village · Brooklyn Bridge · Central Park*

4 days and 24 stops — Mar 12–15, 2026.

## Day 1: Downtown Initiation

*Washington Square*

## Now Now NoHo — Check In

*🏨 4:00 PM · Arrive*

338 Bowery, NoHo. Drop your bags, freshen up. You're 10 mins walk from everything.

*💡 Uber from LGA ~40 min*

## Washington Square Fountain

*⛲ 4:30 PM · Park*

The iconic arch and fountain at the heart of NYU's campus. Golden hour hits the arch beautifully — worth lingering.

*💡 10-min walk from your hotel*

## Day 2: Lower Manhattan & Brooklyn

*Ghostbusters Firehouse · Think Coffee · 9/11 Memorial · Flatiron Building · Tappo · Empire State Building · Madison Square Garden*

Meeting near the hotel late morning, then head downtown for the ferry and Financial District. Dinner at Tappo near the hotel, then evening in the Village. Save Brooklyn and uptown for other days.

## Ghostbusters Firehouse — Hook & Ladder 8

*📺 8:00 AM · TV Spot*

The firehouse from *Ghostbusters*! 14 N Moore St in Tribeca. Still a working firehouse — the Ghostbusters logo mural is often on display inside.

*💡 Quick photo op · Head back to NoHo after for the meeting · Still an active FDNY station*

## Think Coffee — Work Session

*💻 11:00 AM · Work*

Popular NoHo café at 248 Mercer St. Spacious seating, reliable WiFi, great coffee — perfect for a meeting. Steps from your hotel.

*💡 🕐 11 AM – 1 PM · 3-min walk from Now Now NoHo · Grab lunch at one of the many spots on Bleecker after*

## 9/11 Memorial & Museum

*🕊 1:30 PM · Memorial*

Two massive reflecting pools where the towers stood. Walk through the memorial and take in the reflecting pools.

*💡 🎟 Pre-book online · Closed Tuesdays · Short walk from Battery Park*

## Flatiron Building & Madison Square Park

*🏛 6:30 PM · Icon*

The iconic triangular Flatiron Building at 175 5th Ave — one of NYC's most photographed landmarks. Stroll through Madison Square Park right next door before dinner.

*💡 Quick 10-min stop · Right next to Tappo · Great photo ops at sunset*

## Tappo — Thin Crust Pizza

*🍕 7:00 PM · Food*

NYC Thin Crust Pizza at 32 E 21st St in the Flatiron District. Impossibly thin, crispy crust — a local favourite.

*💡 🕐 7 – 8 PM · 15-min walk from your hotel · Reservations recommended*

## Empire State Building

*🏛 8:30 PM · Viewpoint*

Classic Art Deco skyscraper — 86th floor observatory for 360° views. Stunning at night when lit up. A short walk from Tappo.

*💡 🎟 Book skip-the-line tickets · 10-min walk from Tappo · Night views are the best*

## Madison Square Garden

*🏟 9:30 PM · Landmark*

The world's most famous arena — home of the Knicks and Rangers. Even if there's no game, the exterior and surrounding Penn Station area buzz with energy at night.

*💡 5-min walk from Empire State Building · Check if there's an event on*

## Day 3: Brooklyn Morning → West Side Midtown → Uptown Museums

*Brooklyn Bridge · DUMBO · Gleasons Gym · Katz's Deli · High Line · The Edge · McGee's Pub (HIMYM) · MoMA · Rockefeller · LEGO Store · Grand Central · MET · Guggenheim · Central Park*

Early start — walk the Brooklyn Bridge and explore DUMBO before the crowds. Back to Manhattan for Katz's breakfast, then west to the High Line → Hudson Yards → McGee's → MoMA → Rockefeller/LEGO Store → Grand Central. Afternoon: Museum Mile (MET + Guggenheim). Evening: Central Park, Times Square, then Village.

## Walk the Brooklyn Bridge

*🌉 7:00 AM · Walk*

Early morning walk across the bridge — peaceful before the crowds. Gothic towers and steel cables are incredible in the morning light.

*💡 📍 Subway from NoHo (Broadway-Lafayette → Chambers St) · Start from City Hall Park side*

## DUMBO Piers — Manhattan Skyline

*🌆 7:45 AM · Views*

Best view of Manhattan anywhere — the skyline framed by bridge cables. Beautiful in the early morning light.

*💡 📸 Washington St for the iconic bridge shot*

## Gleasons Gym

*🥊 8:15 AM · Experience*

Legendary Brooklyn boxing gym — Muhammad Ali, Mike Tyson trained here. Quick visit while in DUMBO.

*💡 Opens early · 15-min walk from the bridge*

## Katz's Delicatessen

*🥪 9:00 AM · Food*

The most famous deli in New York — pastrami on rye, towering sandwiches, and a room that hasn't changed in decades. The "I'll have what she's having" spot from When Harry Met Sally.

*💡 5-min walk from your hotel · Split a sandwich — they're massive · Cash or card*

## The High Line

*🛤 10:00 AM · Walk*

Elevated rail converted to a linear park. Walk from Meatpacking north to Hudson Yards. ~1.5 miles.

*💡 Open until 8 PM · Great coffee carts along the way*

## The Edge, Hudson Yards

*☁️ 10:20 AM · Viewpoint*

100 floors up, glass-floored outdoor deck. Better than One World Trade — you see all the iconic buildings.

*💡 🎟 Book ahead · Midday light is excellent*

## How I Met Your Mother — McGee's Pub

*📺 10:40 AM · TV Spot*

The real-life bar that inspired MacLaren's Pub. 240 W 55th St in Midtown. The interior is decorated with HIMYM memorabilia — booth seating, show posters, and themed cocktails.

*💡 Quick drink or photo · Right in Midtown · Short walk to MoMA*

## Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)

*🎨 11:00 AM · Museum*

Starry Night, Warhols, Picasso. One of the world's greatest modern art collections.

*💡 Free on Friday evenings · Right next to Rockefeller Center*

## Rockefeller Center & LEGO Store

*🏙 12:15 PM · Landmark*

Art Deco complex with Top of the Rock observation deck. The flagship LEGO Store at 30 Rockefeller Plaza — massive NYC-themed LEGO builds, Build-a-Minifigure, Pick-a-Brick wall, and Mosaic Maker.

*💡 Great for souvenirs · All walkable from MoMA*

## Grand Central Terminal

*🚂 1:15 PM · Landmark*

Beaux-Arts masterpiece — the main concourse ceiling is one of NYC's great interiors. The Whispering Gallery and the Oyster Bar downstairs are worth a detour.

*💡 📍 5-min walk from Rockefeller Center · Free to enter · Look up at the celestial ceiling mural*

## The Metropolitan Museum of Art

*🎨 2:30 PM · Museum*

Plan 2 hours. Egyptian wing, European paintings, Greek sculpture. Pick 2–3 sections and go deep.

*💡 🗺 Grab a floor map · Closed Wednesdays*

## Guggenheim Museum

*🖼 4:30 PM · Museum*

Frank Lloyd Wright's spiral masterpiece. Right next to the MET on the Upper East Side.

*💡 Walk the spiral from top down · 5-min walk from MET*

## Central Park

*🌿 5:30 PM · Park*

843 acres of green in the most vertical city. Bethesda Terrace, the Reservoir, Strawberry Fields.

*💡 👟 Walk south through the park toward Midtown*

## Bonus Suggestions

*Rockettes · Joker Stairs*

## Day 4: Departure

*Lokesh-Anupriya's Place · Newark*

## Lokesh-Anupriya's Place

*🏠 12:00 PM · Visit*

444 Washington Blvd, Apt 5341, Jersey City.

*💡 PATH train from Manhattan or Uber across the river*

## Head to Newark

*🚕 7:00 PM*

Allow ~1–1.5 hours to Newark Liberty Airport from Jersey City. For a 9:50 PM international flight, aim to be at the airport by 7:30–8:00 PM.

*💡 Uber recommended · Short ride from Jersey City*
